## The session price calculator

Most photographers price a session by intuition and then discover the hourly rate was below minimum wage. The calculator makes the arithmetic explicit:

1. **Fixed monthly costs** — rent, software, insurance, gear depreciation, your salary.
2. **Session costs** — travel, an assistant, a second shooter, props.
3. **Equipment** — what you bought for this kind of shoot, amortized.
4. **Time** — shooting, editing, admin and the hours you actually spend per session.
5. **Tasks per month** — how many sessions, edits and admin hours you really do.

The output is a suggested session price with the effective hourly rate next to it. If the rate looks low, the price is low, and now you can see why.

VAT and IRPF are applied transparently, so the number you read is the number you can invoice, not a pre-tax guess.

Everything can be reset to defaults from the calculator itself.

## The black screen

Press the button, or `Ctrl+B` / `⌘B` anywhere in the app, and the screen goes fully black. It is meant for:

- cleaning the monitor;
- using the screen as a light source or a reflector on a shoot;
- keeping the computer awake without the display on.

Press any key or move the mouse to come back. Nothing is closed, nothing sleeps, and it works from any page in the panel.

## Both are free on every plan

There is no paywall behind these tools. They are part of the app because they are useful during a session, not because they are a product to sell.
