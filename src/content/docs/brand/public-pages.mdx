## Two public pages

- **Your profile** at `/@yourhandle`: a landing page with your logo, name, bio, social links, payment button and a link to the portfolio.
- **Your portfolio** at the same address: the galleries you chose to publish, visible to anyone.

Both are the pages a client or a potential client sees when you send one link. Your panel, in contrast, is always the Picstome interface, whichever colors you choose.

## What to put on the profile

- Your **logo**, ideally a transparent PNG or WebP. Two variants, light and dark, keep it readable on both.
- A short **bio**, not a CV.
- Your **social links**, which become the "one link in bio" for Instagram.
- An **email or contact link** for enquiries.

The point is a single URL you can put in a bio, on a card or in an email signature.

## The portfolio

The portfolio is a **curated** selection, not a dump of every gallery. Add a gallery to it when it represents your work:

| | Shared gallery | Portfolio gallery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who sees it | The client with the link | Anyone |
| Password | Possible | Not applicable |
| Purpose | Deliver a shoot | Show your work |
| Expiry | Depends on plan | Never expires |

A gallery can be public without being in the portfolio, and private photos can be [archived](/culling/archiving/) so they disappear from both.

## Branding applies here

Everything under *Branding* changes the **public** pages only:

- **General:** studio name, handle, contact and social links.
- **Logos:** the logo shown in the header and the icon used as favicon.
- **Styling:** the accent color used across the public pages.
- **Watermark:** the mark applied to shared gallery photos.

The panel keeps the Picstome palette on purpose, so a team member never confuses the internal tool with a client-facing page.

## White label

On the subscription, the `Powered by Picstome` footer disappears from the public pages. That is the only branding difference between plans.

## Turning it off

The portfolio can be disabled without deleting anything: the galleries stay in your panel, the public page stops listing them. The profile itself stays available for the bio links.
