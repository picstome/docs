## The public address

Your profile lives at **`/@yourhandle`**. That handle is the address you put in an Instagram bio, on a card or in an email signature. It is unique within Picstome.

If you change the handle, the old address stops working. If you had already shared it, tell people or hand out the new link.

## What to put on it

- **Bio.** One or two sentences about what you do and who for. It is not a résumé; it is what someone needs to decide whether to write to you.
- **Bio links.** Buttons below the bio: bookings, rates, WhatsApp, a PDF portfolio. You can reorder them by dragging.
- **Social links.** Instagram, website and email. They become the icons at the bottom of the profile.

The **View profile** button opens the public page in another tab. **Styling** takes you to the public page colours.

## The brand

The *Brand* settings change only the **public pages**, not the panel:

- **General:** studio name, handle, contact and socials.
- **Logos:** the header logo and the icon used as a favicon.
- **Styling:** the accent colour of the public pages.
- **Watermark:** the one applied to photos in shared galleries. It has its own article.

The panel keeps the Picstome palette on purpose: a team member should never confuse the internal tool with a client page.

If you have public galleries, the **View Portfolio** button appears. If not, the profile still works as a link page.

## The payment button

When you enable it, a visitor can send you a payment from the page itself. The charge goes through Stripe; if you have not connected it, the button does not appear. Setup is in [Getting paid with Stripe](/money/stripe/).

## White-label

With a subscription, the `Powered by Picstome` footer disappears from the public pages. It is the only branding difference between plans; the rest of the profile is the same.
