## How to create an invoice

Three steps. Until the last one, what you have is a draft and it consumes no number.

<Steps>

1. **Client.** Search a record by name or tax ID, or create a new one. If the client is missing fiscal data, the app warns you before letting you continue.

2. **Line items.** Add a description, quantity and price. You can add or remove lines; the total recalculates by itself.

3. **Review and issue.** Choose the invoice type, dates, VAT, IRPF withholding, discount, payment terms and notes. Check the number and press **Issue**.

</Steps>

<Screenshot src={createInvoice} alt="Step 3 of the invoice wizard with the suggested number, the totals summary and the Issue button" caption="Step 3 is the only one that issues: until then everything is still a draft." />

The number on step 3 is a suggestion. You can change it once, when issuing, to match a format you already use (`INV-0042`, `2026/0042`). If it is lower than the last number or leaves a gap, the app warns you but does not block you.

## After issuing

<Screenshot src={issuedInvoice} alt="An issued invoice detail with the payment state, issuer and client details, the public link and the buttons to rectify or duplicate" caption="An issued invoice is a document: from here you send it, get paid or rectify it." />

An issued invoice is no longer edited like a draft. From its page you can:

- **Send it by email** to the client, with the PDF attached and the public link.
- **Open the public link** to see it the way the client does, or deactivate it when it should no longer be available.
- **Download the PDF**.
- **Mark it as paid** (or back to pending) when you get paid outside Picstome. If the payment comes through Stripe, it is marked automatically.
- **Duplicate it** to prepare next month's invoice with the same client and lines. The copy starts as a draft and does not copy the number.
- **Rectify it** if an issued document has a mistake. The original is not modified: you issue a [corrective invoice](/money/vat/).

The only things editable after issuing are the notes and the payment state. If the change affects amounts, fiscal data or line items, it goes through a corrective invoice.

## Draft, issued, paid

Three states, and only one of them is final:

| State | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | You are preparing it. No number, no PDF, editable, deletable. |
| **Issued** | It has a full number and a PDF. It is a document: editing is limited to the notes and the payment state. |
| **Paid** | The same document with the payment recorded. |

Issuing is the moment that matters. A draft consumes no number, so you can prepare ten drafts and only the ones you issue take a number.

## Numbering

Numbers follow **series + year + consecutive**, for example `F-2026-0001`. You configure the series in *Invoice settings*.

- Drafts do not consume numbers.
- The number is assigned when you issue, in order, with no gaps.
- You can edit the printed number once, at issue time, to match a format you already use (`INV-0042`, `2026/0042`). The internal series/year/consecutive and the verification chain are not affected.
- Two series are normal: `F` for invoices and `R` for [corrective invoices](/money/vat/).

## What the client receives

An email with a link to a public page, in your branding, with the invoice and a PDF download. No account and no login.

The page can be deactivated. Deactivating it makes the link useless while the invoice stays in your panel.

## The legal part

Every invoice keeps a **snapshot** of your fiscal data and the client's at the moment of issue. Changing your address or the client's data later never rewrites an invoice already issued.

The PDF carries a verification link to a public page with a fingerprint, so anyone can confirm the document has not been altered.

Picstome is not a certified invoicing system (it does not print VERI*FACTU or the AEAT QR). It produces correct invoices; it is not a substitute for a homologated program if the law requires one in your case.

## Monthly limit

The free plan includes one invoice per month. Drafts count when they are issued, so a month with only drafts consumes nothing.
