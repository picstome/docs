## Draft, issued, paid

Three states, and only one of them is final:

| State | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | You are preparing it. No number, no PDF, editable, deletable. |
| **Issued** | It has a full number and a PDF. It is a document: editing is limited to the notes and the payment state. |
| **Paid** | The same document with the payment recorded. |

Issuing is the moment that matters. A draft consumes no number, so you can prepare ten drafts and only the ones you issue take a number.

## Numbering

Numbers follow **series + year + consecutive**, for example `F-2026-0001`. You configure the series in *Invoice settings*.

- Drafts do not consume numbers.
- The number is assigned when you issue, in order, with no gaps.
- You can edit the printed number once, at issue time, to match a format you already use (`INV-0042`, `2026/0042`). The internal series/year/consecutive and the verification chain are not affected.
- Two series are normal: `F` for invoices and `R` for [corrective invoices](/money/vat/).

## What the client receives

An email with a link to a public page, in your branding, with the invoice and a PDF download. No account and no login.

The page can be deactivated. Deactivating it makes the link useless while the invoice stays in your panel.

## The legal part

Every invoice keeps a **snapshot** of your fiscal data and the client's at the moment of issue. Changing your address or the client's data later never rewrites an invoice already issued.

The PDF carries a verification link to a public page with a fingerprint, so anyone can confirm the document has not been altered.

Picstome is not a certified invoicing system (it does not print VERI*FACTU or the AEAT QR). It produces correct invoices; it is not a substitute for a homologated program if the law requires one in your case.

## Sending and reminders

- The invoice can be sent by email from the invoice page.
- The client download is a PDF regardless of the device.
- The payment state is manual unless the invoice is paid through Stripe, in which case it is marked automatically.

## Monthly limit

The free plan includes one invoice per month. Drafts count when they are issued, so a month with only drafts consumes nothing.
