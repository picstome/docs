## Template vs. contract

This is the concept that trips people up:

| | Template | Contract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | Reusable wording, no client attached | A copy of a template for one client |
| Editable after sending | Yes, and it changes nothing already sent | No, it is frozen |
| Has a signature | No | Yes |
| Has a PDF | No | Yes |

Edit the template whenever you want; new contracts pick up the new text. Contracts already sent keep the text they had.

## The four included templates

Picstome ships four base templates, in English and Spanish. They are not in your *Contract templates* list (that list is for your own templates): they appear under **Use template**, in the *Picstome contract templates* group, when you create a contract or one of your own templates.

<Screenshot src={templatesPicker} alt="The template picker with the Picstome contract templates group and the legal warning" caption="The four base templates appear in your language when you create a contract or a template." />

| Template | What it does | When to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Model Release** | The person photographed, or their guardian if they are a minor, authorizes the use of their image and data. It includes a waiver of privacy and publicity claims and a data protection consent. | Portraits, models, identifiable people in campaigns or portfolios. It does not replace the service contract. |
| **Property Release** | The owner or person responsible for the location authorizes photographing the property and using the images. It confirms there are no restrictions and excludes third-party trademarks. | Private locations, venues, estates, a client's interiors. |
| **TFP Agreement** | A trade-for-portfolio collaboration with no fee. Both parties get a usage license, with credit, and the agreement covers what happens if someone cancels. | Test shoots, collaborations with models or creators, a session exchanged for images. |
| **Brand Usage License** | The client gets a non-exclusive, worldwide, one-year license to use the images in its marketing. The photographer keeps the copyright and portfolio use; there is no sublicensing. | Campaigns and commercial work for brands, products or agencies. |

When you pick one, the text is copied into the contract. You can edit it there; the base template does not change. If you use it from *Contract templates*, it is copied into one of your own templates, which you can save and reuse.

:::caution[Not legal advice]
The templates are a starting point, not legal advice. Picstome is not a law firm and is not responsible for the content. Every country has different laws: review and adapt them before use, especially with minors, sensitive data, restricted properties or third-party trademarks. For real client work, combine the template with your own service contract.
:::

The base templates do not count against the monthly limit: the limit counts contracts **sent**. Use them as often as you like.

## Sending one

<Steps>

1. **Create the contract** and pick a template. The text is copied into the contract.
2. **Fill the details:** client, photoshoot, price and whatever the template expects you to complete.
3. **Send the signing link.** The client opens it in the browser, reads, signs with the mouse or a finger and confirms.
4. **The PDF is generated** with both signatures and stored in the contract. You can download it at any time.

</Steps>

## What the client experiments

A clean page with the contract text, your branding, the price and a signature area. No account. When they sign, they immediately get the signed document.

There is a second signature path for two signers: you sign first, then the client. The order is recorded in the PDF.

## The PDF is the document

The PDF is generated when the contract is signed, not when it is created, and it stays attached to the contract. Downloading it later gives the same file, so you can archive it or attach it to an email without regenerating anything.

If you edit the contract before it is signed, the PDF is not generated yet and the client sees the updated text. Once signed, editing is disabled.

## Monthly limit

The free plan includes one contract per month. The limit counts contracts **sent**, not templates, and it resets with the calendar month. Templates are unlimited on every plan.

## Where to find things

- *Contracts* lists everything you sent, with its state (draft, sent, signed).
- *Contract templates* is where the reusable text lives. The four Picstome templates are picked with **Use template** from a contract or from one of your templates.
- The customer page shows the contracts linked to that client.
