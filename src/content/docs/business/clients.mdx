## One record, everything attached

A customer is the common thread: every gallery, contract and invoice can point to one. That is what makes the client portal and the invoice history possible.

A customer holds:

- the **name** you use internally and the **legal name** that goes on invoices;
- **tax ID, address, city, state, postal code and country**, required to issue a complete invoice;
- contact data, phone and birthday (birthday powers the reminder on the dashboard);
- the galleries, contracts and invoices linked to them.

The tax ID is unique per team, so you cannot create two customers with the same ID by mistake.

## The client portal

Each customer has a private link that lists **all** their shared galleries in one page. It is what you send to a client who has three sessions with you this year: one link instead of three.

What the portal shows:

- every shared gallery of that customer;
- only the galleries whose password has already been entered;
- a page in your branding, with your logo and colors.

What it never shows: invoices, contracts, your internal notes, other customers or the panel itself.

## Fiscal data, before you need it

Fill the legal data **before** creating the invoice, not after. An issued invoice takes a snapshot of the customer's data: correcting the customer later does not change an invoice that has already been issued. To fix that you need a [corrective invoice](/money/vat/).

For a private individual, the tax ID can be their DNI/NIF. For a company, it is the CIF/VAT number and the legal name must match the registry.

## Birthdays and follow-up

The dashboard lists customers with a birthday in the next days. It is a small feature on purpose: a reminder, not a marketing campaign. Nothing is sent automatically.

## Deleting a customer

Deleting a customer does not delete the galleries, contracts or invoices already created: they keep their own copy of the data. The customer record disappears from your lists.
