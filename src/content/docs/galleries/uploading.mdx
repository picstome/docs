## What you can upload

| Type | Formats |
| --- | --- |
| Images | JPG, JPEG, PNG, TIFF |
| RAW | CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, DNG, ORF, RW2, PEF, SRW, MOS, MRW, 3FR |
| Video | MP4, WEBM, OGG |
| Documents | PDF |

The limit is **5 GB per file**. Anything above it is skipped with a message listing which files were left out; the rest of the batch still uploads.

## What happens after the upload

Uploading and processing are two different steps, and this is where the confusion starts:

1. The file lands in storage.
2. It is processed: resized, a small grid thumbnail generated, and RAW files are converted to JPEG.
3. The photo becomes visible in the gallery.

While this runs, the photo shows as *processing*. If you reload the page the list is correct, but a photo may take a few seconds to appear with its thumbnail. A large video takes longer than a photo.

## Duplicates become versions

If you upload a file whose name already exists in the gallery, Picstome does **not** silently replace the old one. It asks. You can [save it as a new version](/galleries/file-versions/), replace the current one or skip it.

For RAW files, the versioning is automatic: `IMG_0001.CR2` and a later `IMG_0001.JPG` share the same identity.

## The upload panel

Drop files anywhere on the gallery page, or use the upload button. A panel opens in the bottom corner and stays there until the batch is done:

<Screenshot src={dockScreenshot} alt="The upload panel with the overall progress, the time left and each file's progress" caption="Three files are finishing, the rest are still uploading. The panel stays while you move around Picstome." />

- **Overall progress** in bytes, with an estimate of the time left.
- **Each file** with its own progress bar. A file that fails can be retried from the panel without starting the batch again.
- **Files that already exist** are listed together, so you can choose *new version*, *replace* or *skip* once and apply it to all of them.
- **Minimize** shrinks the panel to a small chip; the upload keeps running and you can expand it again at any time.
- When everything has finished, a summary tells you how many photos landed in which gallery, with a link back to it.

Files travel **straight to storage** from your browser, not through the Picstome server, which is why large videos up to the 5 GB limit upload without problems.

## You can keep working while it uploads

The panel belongs to Picstome, not to the gallery page. You can open another gallery, write an invoice or check a contract and the upload keeps going in the corner.

What stops it is leaving Picstome: closing the tab, reloading the page or closing the browser. If there are uploads in progress, the browser warns you first. Files that had already finished stay in the gallery; only the pending ones stop.

To stop a batch on purpose, press the **X** in the panel. Picstome asks for confirmation and tells you how many files are left. The ones already uploaded are kept.

For very large shoots, [camera upload](/galleries/direct-upload/) is a better path than dragging files into the browser.

## When an upload is rejected

- **Files too large:** over the 5 GB per-file limit.
- **Unsupported file type:** the extension is not in the list above.
- **Not enough storage:** the plan has no room left. Free space or upgrade; nothing is partially stored.
- **Pixel limit:** with *original size* enabled, a gallery rejects images above the maximum pixel count. Disable it for that gallery if you need to keep huge files.
