Sharing is the moment the gallery becomes something a client can open. It is a single link, no account and no app, and the switches in the *Share* tab decide exactly what happens when somebody clicks it.

## What sharing is not

- **It is not a login.** The client does not create an account. Whoever has the link can open the gallery, which is why there is a password switch.
- **It is not publishing.** A shared gallery is still private by default: it is not indexed, not in your portfolio, and not visible to anyone without the link.
- **It is not permanent.** You can turn sharing off at any time and the link stops working immediately, while the gallery and the client's selection stay in your panel.

## Before you send it

1. **Look at the gallery as the client will see it.** The client page is reachable from the preview before you share it.
2. **Decide the switches once.** Password, selection, downloads, watermark and comments each have a sensible default, and your last choice is remembered for the next gallery.
3. **Check the count.** If the gallery has photos still processing they appear a few seconds later, so wait before sending a link to a client who is waiting.

## After you send it

Your panel shows the client's activity as it happens: favourites, comments and the selection limit notification. Nothing is sent by email unless you enable it, so a quiet gallery means a client who has not opened the link yet, not a broken link.
