## The whole flow

<Steps>

1. **Create the gallery.** In *Galleries*, press the create button, give it a name and, if you want, link it to a photoshoot and pick an expiration date.

2. **Upload the photos.** Drag the files onto the gallery. Uploading happens in the background: you can leave the page and come back.

3. **Share it.** Open the *Share* tab, switch sharing on, and copy the link. That link is what your client opens.

4. **Let the client work.** They open the link in any browser, no account and no app. They can favorite photos, comment, or download, depending on the switches you enabled.

5. **Read the result.** Favorites appear in the *Favorites* tab of the gallery. From there you can export the list of file names.

</Steps>

## The three decisions that matter

Most of the confusion on the first gallery comes from three switches. Everything else has a sensible default.

| Switch | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| **[Original size](/galleries/original-size/)** | Whether the gallery keeps the full files or web-sized versions. |
| **[Selection limit](/sharing/selection/)** | Whether the client can favorite a maximum number of photos. |
| **[Download](/sharing/downloads/)** | Whether the client can download individual photos or a ZIP of the whole gallery. |

## What the client receives

Nothing like a login form. The link opens a page with your logo, the gallery name and the photos. If you protected it with a password, they type it once and the browser remembers it for that gallery.

There is no sign-up, no email confirmation and no app to install. A client who loses the link can get it again from you at any time: sharing the gallery again reuses the same link.

## Common first-time questions

**Do I have to upload the RAW files?** No. Upload the JPEG/PNG/TIFF you want to show, or let the camera upload send the JPEG over FTP. RAW files are accepted, but they are heavy and the client never downloads them from a shared gallery.

**Can I change the photos after sending the link?** Yes. Adding, replacing or archiving photos updates the gallery the client sees. A photo you archive disappears from their view immediately.

**What happens when the gallery expires?** The client can no longer open it and the files are removed. The [plans article](/start/plans-and-storage/) explains the windows.
