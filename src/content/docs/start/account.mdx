## Signing in without a password

<Screenshot src={loginScreenshot} alt="The login page with the email field, the Log in button and the Log in with password link" caption="Type your email and press Log in: the link arrives by email. The password option is right below." />

Picstome does not ask you for a password to create an account. To sign in, type your email on the login page and Picstome sends you a **login link**. Open it and you are in, on the page you were trying to reach.

- The link works **once** and expires after **15 minutes**. If it has expired, request a new one from the login page.
- If the email does not arrive, check the spam folder. The login page shows the address it was sent to, so you can spot a typo.
- Emails Picstome sends you (a client comment, a contract signed, a gallery about to expire…) include their own button to sign in directly. Those links last 24 hours.

## Setting a password (optional)

If you prefer not to wait for an email, create a password in **Settings → Password**. From then on the login page lets you choose: *Log in with password* or *Use a login link instead*. Both keep working.

You can change the password from the same screen at any time.

## Deleting your account

<Screenshot src={deleteScreenshot} alt="The Delete account dialog with the inventory of galleries, photos, storage, contracts, invoices and clients" caption="The dialog lists what will be deleted and warns about issued invoices before you confirm." />

The **Delete account** button is at the bottom of **Settings → Profile** (and in the danger zone of **Settings → Password**). Before confirming, Picstome shows you what is about to disappear: galleries, photos and videos, storage in use, contracts, invoices and clients.

To confirm you type your email, and your current password if you have one.

What happens next:

- **Everything is removed**, from the database and from storage: galleries, photos, versions, contracts and signatures, clients, invoices and payments, your public profile and portfolio. It cannot be undone.
- **Issued invoices are fiscal records.** Download them first: after deletion they cannot be recovered or downloaded again.
- **An active subscription is cancelled immediately**, without a refund for the remaining time. A lifetime plan is removed as well.
- **Camera upload stops working**, and the credentials saved in your camera become invalid.
- **Connected apps lose access** straight away.

You are signed out as soon as you confirm; the cleanup itself runs in the background and can take a few minutes for a large account.
