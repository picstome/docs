## Archive is not delete

This is the single most important distinction in a gallery:

| | Archive | Delete |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photo visible to client | No | No |
| File on disk | Kept | Removed |
| Can be undone | Yes, instantly | No |
| Counts against storage | Yes | No |

Archive a photo when you want it out of the client's view for now: a duplicate, a blink, a shot you may still want, a photo kept for the record. Nothing is uploaded again and nothing is lost.

Delete a photo only when you are sure. It removes the file and the row.

## What the client notices

Nothing but the absence. Archived photos disappear from the shared gallery, from the ZIP download, from the portfolio grid, and their photo page returns a *not found*. Nobody gets an email saying a photo was archived.

## Where archived photos live

The owner sees an **Archived** tab in the gallery, right after *Photos*, and only while at least one photo is archived. From there you can review, restore or delete.

The photographer viewer has an `A` shortcut that toggles the current photo. Pressing it on a live photo archives it and jumps on; pressing it in the Archived tab restores it.

## Deleting everything archived

The Archived tab has a **Delete all archived photos** action. It is deliberately the only destructive action in the gallery that warns in capitals: it removes every archived photo and its files permanently. Use it to finish a cleanup, never as a quick way to empty the tab.

## Common case

You delivered 400 photos, the client selected 60, and the rest are noise. Archiving the 340 leftovers hides them from the client without re-uploading anything, and you can restore any of them if the client asks for "that one with the tree".
